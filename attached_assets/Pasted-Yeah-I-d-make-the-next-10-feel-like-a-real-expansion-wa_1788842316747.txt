# 37. BARBER BRO

### “Everybody need a lineup.”

**Gameplay**
**3 Cost / 4 Power**

**LINE UP**
On Reveal: Give your lowest-Power other card here **+2 Power**. Give the highest-Power enemy here **-1 Power**.

Very simple, very readable, always useful.

**Visual design**

* tall lean Black barber
* crisp white tee under black leather barber apron
* immaculate pants with stacked hem
* gold chain, barber pole pendant
* cordless clippers in one hand
* straight razor case clipped at waist
* folding barber cape draped over one shoulder
* chrome tool case
* fresh low-top sneakers

**Pose**
One hand holding clippers toward camera, other brushing imaginary hairline.

**Palette**
black / cream / chrome / barber-pole red accent

**Silhouette hook**
giant cape + clippers

# 38. BOTTLE GIRL

### “Section secured.”

**LAST CALL**

* clear acrylic purse
* wrist full of club bands
* tiny earpiece radio
* gold hoop earrings

**Pose**

**Palette**
black / champagne gold / bottle green / hot pink detail

**Silhouette hook**
wide glowing bottle tray

# 41. LANDLORD

### “Rent due.”

**Gameplay**
**4 Cost / 5 Power**

**RENT DUE**
Ongoing: The first enemy card played at this district each round costs **1 additional Hype**.

If they cannot afford the surcharge, they cannot play it here.

This gives us real **Hype taxation**.

* oversized camel coat
* dress shirt with rolled sleeves
* massive key ring
* clipboard stuffed with notices
* calculator
* tape measure
* phone on belt
* chunky loafers / work shoes

**Pose**
Holding a giant ring of keys toward camera.

**Palette**
camel / dark brown / brass / faded green

**Silhouette hook**
huge keys + paperwork
