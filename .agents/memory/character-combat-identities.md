name: Character combat identities
description: User-approved character roles and Triple OG placement intent to preserve when tuning combat.
---

Keep Janitor focused on reversing attacks, Homeless Wiseman on predictions and traps, and Squabble House Manager on cheap, direct brawling. Do not turn these characters into interchangeable cleanse-and-protect supports.

**Why:** The user explicitly rejected the initial support interpretations for these three, while approving Juneteenth Chair Guy's combination of a direct hit and protecting a teammate.

**How to apply:** Balance numbers and counterplay without replacing those identities. Preserve Chair Guy's attack-and-protect concept unless the user asks to change it.

The mythical Triple OGs keep their fixed sides: Blue plays only in the left lane, and Red only in the right lane. They must still be playable when a location in their own lane adds a Motion penalty or closes that lane.

**Why:** The user clarified that “any location” meant any location occupying the OG's assigned lane, not permission to choose any lane. The earlier any-lane interpretation was incorrect.

**How to apply:** Preserve home-lane targeting while ignoring location-added Motion penalties and location play locks there. Do not turn this into free placement, general movement immunity, or a rule for legacy side OGs or other cards. Their reveal must still resolve in their home lane.

Keep both dogs and both Triple OGs out of the Squabblehouse staff-deck baseline; they belong in gang decks.

**Why:** The user rejected the mixed staff-and-gang recipe used in the first balance sweep. Its win rates and recommendations do not describe the intended Squabblehouse staff deck, even though the mixed recipe existed in the catalog.

**How to apply:** Do not treat an existing mixed recipe as user-approved faction composition. Establish a legal staff-focused roster before repeating the matchup tests; preserve historical mixed-deck results without reusing them as staff-deck evidence.

Squabblehouse is a play on Waffle House, not a hotel. Its staff concepts should come from a late-night diner and short-order kitchen.

**Why:** The user explicitly corrected hotel-style front-desk and doorman concepts as the wrong inspiration.

**How to apply:** Use diner jobs, food-service equipment, and late-night diner fighting energy when proposing characters, artwork, and abilities. Do not substitute hotel reception, concierge, or velvet-rope venue roles.

A Side of Hands is intended as a support card that grants friendly Hands and weakens an opponent in the same play.

**Why:** The user introduced it specifically as a dual buff-and-debuff support, rather than another staff character.

**How to apply:** Preserve both effects when designing its kit; do not replace the friendly Hands gain with a card draw or make the two effects mutually exclusive.

Keep the diner crew's positioning role distinct from its counter roles. Matchup counter work should not automatically replace Bus Boy's staff relocation or Side of Hands' dual support.

**Why:** After the replacement crew failed to show consistent matchup gains, the user chose targeted Cashier, Griddle Master, and Janitor counters to movement, Protection, and forced removal, rather than the alternative Bus Boy bonus-stripping rework.

**How to apply:** Preserve those separate roles when adjusting the counter package. Treat printed-stat increases or changes to the other staff's jobs as separate balance decisions, not automatic consequences of a disappointing bot result.

Counter additions should preserve the staff's original pressure and defensive role rather than replace them.

**Why:** After a replacement-style counter package produced mixed bot results, the user approved an additive revision: retain the existing attack or denial, and avoid spending its original defensive budget on a newly covered threat.

**How to apply:** When proposing future counter changes, explicitly identify what original value remains and whether the new trigger competes with it. Do not silently trade away targeting, damage-over-time, or unrelated reversal coverage to add a counter.
