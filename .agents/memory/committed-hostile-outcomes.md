---
name: Committed hostile outcomes
description: Why counterable attempts cannot establish success through callback-local flags.
---

Award success-only training from the committed hostile-effect result, not a flag set while constructing the attempted effect. Compare the actual recipient's pre-attempt state with the returned match, including redirected recipients.

**Why:** Reversal hooks may discard attempted state after its callback runs. Callback-local success flags survive that rejection and can grant phantom rewards, especially when a separate passive was already armed and did not change.

**How to apply:** Whenever a hostile ability earns training, refunds, summons, or other success-dependent rewards, cover an already-armed passive plus a reversed new attempt. Include unlocked upgrades and assert both the absence of the attempted effect and the absence of its success reward.
