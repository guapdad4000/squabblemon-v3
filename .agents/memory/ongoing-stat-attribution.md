---
name: Ongoing combat stats and attribution
description: Why ongoing stat values are snapshot-owned and their loss must remain distinct from committed hostile damage.
---

Reconcile board-dependent combat bonuses into immutable authoritative snapshots before targeting, scoring, or death checks. Historical frames and online clients must use their recorded values, including zero for legacy frames that have no ongoing value, rather than calculating from a later board.

Only actual, committed loss from a hostile permanent-modifier change can count as damage, theft, debt, training success, or attacker kill credit. Bound that loss by actual available combat Hands, not a zero-clamped base-only comparison: an ongoing-supported character can have negative permanent Hands and still suffer genuine hostile loss. Losing only an ongoing bonus may still be lethal, but that casualty needs an explicit neutral presentation cause instead of damage or burn feedback.

**Why:** A staff-dependent bonus initially became false enemy damage when an employee departed. Complete replay boards alone also hid the resulting neutral casualty from participant-based online history and animation. A base-only clamp then hid real theft and bypassed harmful-effect defenses on characters kept alive by ongoing Hands.

**How to apply:** Keep permanent modifications and ongoing contributions separate in new mechanics, snapshots, and inspectors. Test a mixed packet that kills its direct target and indirectly removes an aura-dependent ally; preserve the direct hit while explaining the neutral casualty without awarding attacker credit. Capture that committed transition before damage watchers react rather than treating any nested event as proof it was captured. Also test genuine theft and reversal against a living target with nonpositive permanent Hands and a positive ongoing contribution.
