---
name: New-card guidance
description: Collection-only discovery guidance and why missing viewing history cannot mark an entire inventory new.
---

New-card guidance is presentation only. Never change a deck, recruit cards, or treat a missing viewing history as proof that the whole collection is new.

**Why:** The feature was introduced without a pre-existing last-viewed marker. Touring the entire established collection on rollout or on a new browser would interrupt the player for too long.

**How to apply:** Use a conservative recent-acquisition starting point on the first visit, then track unseen ownership changes per player. Keep guidance interruptible and separate from onboarding. Seen state is intentionally browser-local; cross-device synchronization would need an explicit account-level viewing marker, not the account's last login time.

Keep automatic new-card highlights on Collection, not Deck Builder, and do not add a countdown, progress, or pause panel.

**Why:** The user found the tour distracting while editing a deck and explicitly redirected it to Collection. The desired presentation is the individual card highlight, not another interface module.

**How to apply:** Keep deck editing stable. On Collection, use unobtrusive card-level cues and stop automatic movement when the player interacts.
