---
name: Progression rollout boundaries
description: Preserve finite story promises and distinguish organic pacing from promotional wallets.
---
Finite story-card duplicate rewards deliberately retain their historical shard award rather than adopting repeatable pack duplicate rates.

**Why:** Story rewards can already be issued inside immutable encounter snapshots; silently changing their conversion policy would change an existing promise. Their finite supply is not the repeatable shard inflation source.

**How to apply:** Treat future pack tuning separately from historical story receipts, keep descriptions based on persisted receipt amounts, and preserve reward claim identities.

Organic economy simulations must exclude promotional wallets and identify those grants separately.

**Why:** Production-available promotional grants can dwarf weeks of ordinary play. Source findings alone do not establish abuse or describe any live player's account.

**How to apply:** Use immutable historical fixtures for before/after simulations, not a moving Git HEAD. Clearly distinguish mocked browser journeys, isolated database verification, and real authenticated production evidence.
