---
name: Reward broadcast boundaries
description: Confirmation and presentation-time constraints for optional reward footage.
---

Battle reward broadcasts may use only the time remaining in the existing terminal presentation hold after a successful completion response. If saving finishes after that window, show rewards normally without a late cinematic.

**Why:** The user required both confirmed reward presentation and unchanged result timing. Waiting for a save and then playing a full clip would add a mandatory delay; playing before confirmation could celebrate a failed save.

**How to apply:** Start saving immediately, keep result navigation independent, and permit a selected short clip only within that match's live presentation window. Retry, restored matches, review and reopening must not create another window.

Generic reward broadcasts require explicit producer context and context-neutral object footage, not inferred titles or an opponent defeat. Existing Clout collection, shop training, onboarding, battle haul and level ceremonies keep their own climax.

**Why:** The supplied montages include face-offs, threats and knockout shots that can wrongly imply a fight happened. The user explicitly deferred openings and rejected duplicate ceremonies.

**How to apply:** Audit footage as combat outcomes versus neutral inserts, opt eligible confirmed producers in, and preserve the receipt queue's distinction between revealing the current receipt and dismissing it.
