---
name: Story-earned cards
description: Why story fighters are playable collection cards but excluded from ordinary random acquisition.
---

Story fighters should be real catalog cards usable in owned crews, awarded alongside—not instead of—their story-character unlocks. Keep ordinary packs from granting them before the authored story milestone, and do not reveal their unearned variants as pack bonuses.

**Why:** A story portrait unlock does not grant collection ownership or deck legality. A random card or variant obtained early would also bypass or spoil the narrative reward.

**How to apply:** New story-exclusive cards need server-authoritative, versioned card claims for both first clears and previously cleared accounts. Exclude the cards from general draw pools and their variants until owned, but keep saved ownership and decks valid.
