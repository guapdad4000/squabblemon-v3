---
name: Battle Motion curve
description: The durable economy rule that keeps early rounds playable and late rounds consequential.
---

The shared six-round battle economy starts both crews at 2 Motion. On each later round, the round number is the refill baseline, at most 1 unspent Motion carries forward, and total Motion is capped at 6.

**Why:** A 1-Motion opening created dead turns for valid starter crews, while unlimited banking made later rounds automatic. Carrying exactly one point gives passing strategic tempo without allowing a runaway resource stockpile.

**How to apply:** Preserve this shape across Tutorial, Training, story, and reward-verified matches. Story modifiers may change resources explicitly, but normal balance changes should keep a playable opening, bounded carryover, and transcript determinism.
