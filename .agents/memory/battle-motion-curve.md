---
name: Battle Motion curve
description: The durable economy rule that keeps early rounds playable and late rounds consequential.
---

Preserve a playable opening and bounded carryover when tuning individual cards; do not use a crew balance patch to change the combat economy.

**Why:** A 1-Motion opening created dead turns for valid starter crews, while unlimited banking made later rounds automatic. Carrying exactly one point gives passing strategic tempo without allowing a runaway resource stockpile.

**How to apply:** Preserve this shape across Tutorial, Training, story, and reward-verified matches. Story modifiers may change resources explicitly. Read the current authoritative constants rather than restoring the older 6-Motion cap from historical notes; that cap is obsolete.
