---
name: Supplied clip identity
description: Matching opaque animation files to existing characters without confusing move titles with character identities.
---

Compare unidentified supplied clips against the existing roster portraits, using first, middle, and late frames. An embedded move title that differs from the printed character name does not mean the clip belongs to an unrelated character.

**Why:** Two supplied OG solo clips were initially left unmatched because their move title and generic outfit descriptions were treated as character identities. Comparing faces, clothing, and accessories against the existing portraits established the matches.

**How to apply:** Before calling an opaque clip unrelated or leaving it unassigned, check both the requested new characters and the existing roster. This matters especially when companions are being separated into their own playable cards.
