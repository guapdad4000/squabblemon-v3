# Four revised practice crews

These are optional workshop recommendations. They do not replace starter decks or edit saved crews.

## Cellblock lane sequence

**Crew:** Inmate Crafty, Inmate Boyfriend, Inmate Informant, Inmate Contraband, Regular Guy named LeBron James, Bust-Down Watch, Cognac Bottle, Rastamon, Wifey, Stud.

Lead with a real support-kind card, then play the 2-Motion / 3-Hands Crafty for its local +2. Contest another district with an inmate before Boyfriend arrives: he can give the weakest other local ally +2 and the weakest inmate in another district +1.

## Sherlock and Watson

**Crew:** Sherlock, Watson, Crossing Guard, Night Medic, Wifey, Counter, Oz, Rastamon, Bust-Down Watch, Tin Man.

Put Sherlock's visible trap where an entrance matters. Only an actual cancellation rewards him and the weakest other friendly character with +2 Hands each. Watson restores up to 3 Hands of actual damage and Protects that injured ally; with no healing target, he Protects the weakest other local ally. He also Protects a friendly Sherlock anywhere.

## Demario and Luigion

**Crew:** Demario, Luigion, Rastamon, Cool Vibe YN, Plug, Bust-Down Watch, Soul Food, Black Cowboy, Hair Stylist, Stylist.

The 2-Motion / 2-Hands Demario makes one local Mushroom when space permits. Normal and Powered Luigion can each consume at most one local Mushroom per deployment for +2 Hands. Normal Luigion makes the pair useful without a gamble; SQUABBLE is optional for the powered jump.

## Dark Counterplay

**Crew:** Counter, Gamer, Goth Kid, Closet Nerd, Red Pill, BUDDY, Wifey, Pinay Nurse, Plug, Bust-Down Watch.

Closet Nerd, BUDDY, Red Pill, and Goth Kid provide Silence or Weaken opportunities. Plan one dependable new debuff per round so Counter and Gamer can trigger their once-per-round Dark payoffs. This recommendation improves the enabler mix; it does not buff Counter or alter a saved crew.
