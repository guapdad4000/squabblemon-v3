## Mailman letter

From: Squabblemon Team

Subject: Patch 1.8: Blood & Crips: Hold Your Block

Patch 1.8 · October 2, 2026
Blood & Crips: Hold Your Block

Blue Set spreads the support. Red Set brings the pressure. This balance update strengthens both crews, gets their dogs moving sooner, and gives you more ways to fight for every district.

Buffs
• CLUE COOKY: Blue Set allies now contribute homage without losing Hands, up to +4 Hands total per reveal. He also gives the weakest Blue Set ally in each other district +2 Hands and Protection.
• Ganger Blue: now costs 2 Motion. Covering a Blue Set ally gives that ally +4 Hands and Protection, and gives Ganger Blue +1 Hand.
• LOOK OUT: calls can rearm at round start with another Blue Set ally. Each call gives your weakest other Blue Set ally +2 Hands alongside the lane discount. Maximum three calls per match, once per round.
• OG Blue: Crossfire gains +1 extra Hand for each other district containing a friendly Blue Set card, up to +2.
• Blue-Nose Pit: supporting its OG also gives your weakest Blue Set ally in another district +2 Hands.
• Cane Corso: now has 3 base Hands, still at 2 Motion.
• Ganger Red: gains +1 Hand when its attack consumes Protection or lands damage. Protection still blocks the damage.
• INITIATION: the next recruit gains +2 Hands and the district’s set color when there is one.

Changes
• Both dogs walk one adjacent lane toward their matching Triple OG when played. They support on arrival and keep walking at round start. Replayed reveals do not grant an extra step.
• RED PUNCH, BLOCK SPINNER, and Cane Corso now reliably earn their existing training bonuses after successful effects, including qualifying delayed effects.
• Base cards are the focus of this balance pass. XP progression and upgrade unlocks are unchanged.
• Build a Blood or Crips crew, swap sides with a friend, and send your match feedback through Events. Tell us which turn swung the game.
