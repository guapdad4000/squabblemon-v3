## Shared runtime contracts required for honest full coverage

1. **Typed lifecycle events.** Separate paid deployment, genuine arrival, successful move, entrance resolution, entrance echo, actual damage, bonus removal, genuine gain, copied gain, refund, death and return. Carry source identity, controller, original paid lane and causal root. After-play effects never fire twice for a moved entrance or echo.
2. **Stable identity and ledgers.** Separate catalog card, persistent instance, current ability identity and controller. Return, transformation, recruitment and copied abilities do not refresh once-per-match or shared-per-side limits. Ledger scopes must be explicit: instance, side, lane, side/lane, pair, target, window, match.
3. **Time conversion.** Breakthrough has continuous Motion and no rounds. Existing adaptations use ten-second gates and periodic hand auras. Publish a single conversion policy for “each round”, “through next round”, “Round 4”, “final round”, delayed returns and After Party. Do not let each new adapter invent unrelated timers. Last Stand stops scheduled draws but cannot silently disable all legal card play; explicitly decide whether authored draws/returns are blocked.
4. **Hands provenance.** Track printed/current base, permanent bonus, actual enemy damage, removable bonus and recoverable damage separately. Confiscation is not damage or healing credit. No overkill payout, no regeneration of confiscated bonus, no positive gain after a blocked/fully absorbed hit.
5. **One hostile-effect pipeline.** Immunity, personal Protection, lane shields, interception, damage prevention, reflection and status application return actual outcomes. Packages such as damage+Weaken or execution+summon obey the authored block boundary. A blocked hit never grants an on-damage reward.
6. **Movement admission.** Centralize legal lateral moves, pairs, swaps, forced moves, locks, curfews, returns and identity-preserving redeploys. Preserve longitudinal progress, prevent jumps over opposing blockers, keep frozen units from stalling allies, and reward only successful arrival. Do not restore normal-mode four-card district caps to Breakthrough's unlimited lanes.
7. **Actions and choices.** Supports and blockbusters are paid actions, never zero-Hands marching fighters. Extend commands with validated choice/investment/wager/target fields. Invalid or stale choices spend nothing and preserve the card; retry identity includes every chosen value. AI uses the same legal action enumeration.
8. **Tokens, hazards and summons.** Define independent templates for Guyana, Stewards, Mushrooms, Fake Marriage, Grin, Card Guards, Elephant and bounded generated clones. Preserve their art and scoring/combat semantics. Tokens never enter packs/decks, don't count as characters where excluded, and cannot each breach the base accidentally unless that conversion is expressly authored. Mushroom/Bud/Bomb are hazards, not extra ordinary fighters.
9. **Echo/copy policy.** Reuse explicit normal-engine eligibility rather than copying any description. `canEchoCreativeEntrance` excludes persistent contracts; Oz, Tayaty, Scammer and Torch must not recurse. Echoes do not fabricate paid plays, arrivals, refund opportunities, hand-aura activations or reset caps.
10. **Private information and reproducible randomness.** Seed gameplay RNG once on the authority; persist its advances. Public reveal events expose only authorized cards, never future queue order or RNG. D6 wagers and bounded clones need deterministic replay, not `Math.random()`.

The normal engine already separates these concerns in `CreativeTools`, `MusicTools`, `selectedAbilities`, `homecomingAbilities`, `streetLegendsAbilities`, `maryMack`, and typed marks/discount tokens. Their pure metadata and eligibility sets are reusable; their `Match` reducers are not drop-in Breakthrough implementations because rounds, board geometry, instance IDs and capacity differ.

## Explicit design blockers to resolve in code and presentation

- **After Party:** The canonical card extends six rounds to seven once; Breakthrough has a 180-second deadline and final-30-second draw lock. Author a one-use deadline/Last Stand extension with visible timer behavior, rather than dropping the extension clause or repeatedly extending the match.
- **The Dice Game:** Both seats must afford a 1–3 Motion wager. No hidden forced negative balance, modal pause of the server clock, reroll on retry or disclosure of seed. Zero-cost printed card does not imply zero wager.
- **The Concert / Homeless Guy:** Explicit choice and investment controls, legal preview and final-state validation are necessary; automatic arbitrary choices are not equivalent usability.
- **The Setup:** Requires two legal friendly characters; the victim is a sacrifice, not enemy damage. Transfer current Hands exactly and spend Protection only according to the hostile package, never on friendly sacrifice.
- **Block Spin:** Retain bounded damage history with event identity/cause and affected survivors. Replayed damage cannot enter its own eligible history.
- **Control / returns:** Pimp Swookie recruitment, forced movement, Alice/Mad Hatter/Dorothy returns, Mary Mack and copied abilities require persistent identity beyond the current card-ID-only hand model.
- **Support presence:** Inmate Crafty's printed support synergy needs a deliberate Breakthrough interpretation because supports resolve as actions. Do not fake a permanent 0-Hands support unit just to satisfy a condition.
- **Existing 45:** Reconcile them too. Canonical traits and multi-clause effects (held elemental bonds, Mama Bear, Ankle Breaker, etc.) must pass the same eight release checks; an approved-looking animation or old registry entry is insufficient proof.
