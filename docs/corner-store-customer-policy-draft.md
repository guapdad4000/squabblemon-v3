## Clout purchases

Purchases are for players aged 18 or older in supported countries. Clout is virtual currency for use within Squabblemon, not cash. It cannot be withdrawn, transferred to another player, or exchanged for money.

The store displays the bundle’s base price and currency. Applicable tax is calculated during Stripe-hosted checkout, which displays the final total before payment. Squabblemon does not collect your full card details. Clout is added to the signed-in account only after payment is verified. Closing checkout or returning to the game does not, by itself, confirm payment.

If a purchase is still processing, check **Fade Market → Your Orders** before attempting another payment. If the payment completed but Clout has not arrived, contact support with the order reference. Do not send payment-card details.

## Refund requests — approved launch terms

When live purchases launch, we will accept refund requests within **14 days of purchase** for an unused Clout bundle. This voluntary request window does not limit any rights or remedies you have under applicable law.

Duplicate charges, incorrect charges, or purchases that were not delivered will be investigated and corrected as appropriate. Please report suspected unauthorized purchases promptly.

If some or all of the purchased Clout has been spent, support will review the circumstances individually. Spending Clout does not remove any refund rights required by law. We do not guarantee a discretionary refund for a used bundle.

Approved refunds are issued through Stripe to the original payment method where supported. Your bank or payment provider determines when the refund appears. We will not ask for another payment or your full card details to process a refund.

A provider refund does not automatically remove Clout from your game wallet. Support will explain any proposed adjustment of refunded purchase entitlements separately. We will not silently remove unrelated earned rewards or create a negative balance.

Nothing in this policy limits mandatory consumer protections, legally required refunds, or your right to dispute a charge with your payment provider.

## Contact support — customer-facing wording

Need help with a Clout purchase? Open **Fade Market → Your Orders** and find the relevant purchase. Contact Squabblemon support using the support link beside checkout.

Include:

- Your order reference.
- A brief description of the problem.
- The approximate purchase date and whether Clout has been received or spent.

Never send a password, sign-in code, full card number, security code, or payment credentials. Support may ask you to verify that you control the purchasing account through a secure process.
