# Season Two editorial and implementation checklist

- Eight chapter IDs exactly match `storySeasons.ts`, in global orders 9–16.
- Chapter 9 requires `the-crown`; each later chapter requires the preceding Season Two chapter.
- 24 substantive battles: three per chapter, with a deliberate mix of 4-, 5-, and 6-round encounters.
- Every battle has exactly three supported star objectives and uses `completeEngineCrew` with real catalog IDs.
- Lane locks, scheduled reinforcements, and multi-phase finales are distributed throughout the season.
- Three short evidence-order puzzles occur in Chapters 9, 13, and 15 (season chapters 1, 5, and 7), attached to non-battle dialogue nodes.
- Chapter 9 uses `assets/story/theater/rooftop.webp`; all puzzles use `assets/story/theater/evidence.webp`. Later scenes reuse confirmed venue art.
- Rewards are modest: 75–125 Street XP per battle, 40 Street XP per puzzle, 125 Street XP and one ticket per normal chapter close, three tickets at the season finale. No cosmetics, character unlocks, or unsolicited cards.
- All dialogue portraits map to existing character assets.
- Card matches only settle explicitly announced contests. Ownership, evidence authenticity, family truth, lease validity, and parenting are resolved outside match results.
- OG Uncle lives. Blue grows without becoming sole authority. Cracked Head's parenting obligations remain specific. Baby Momma and Wifey retain agency.
- The gathering-space threat ends through a countersigned, funded cooperative lease, public-use schedule, repair compliance, partner guarantees, and coordinated teardown.
