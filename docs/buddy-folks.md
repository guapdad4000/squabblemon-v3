# BUDDY and FOLKS

Both are Legendary characters available through the existing Street Pack pool.

| Card | Motion / Hands | Printed ability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BUDDY (Dark) | 3 / 4 | Myth Buster targets the strongest Mythical in his district, otherwise the strongest local enemy. Normal target: -2 Hands. Mythical: -5 Hands and Silence; BUDDY gains +2 when that hit lands. |
| FOLKS (Fire) | 4 / 3 | Whole Block Hot applies 3 Burn to every current enemy across all districts; every other friendly Fire character gains +1 Hand. Fire versus Plant adds the standard +1 Burn. |

BUDDY's damage and Silence are one hit. A shield blocks both, mitigation reduces damage, and uncounterable targets prevent the hit and bonus. Rarity comes from the gameplay catalog, never portrait IDs. Silence stops future abilities; it does not reverse already resolved effects. Successful Mythical knockouts still earn the bonus.

FOLKS has a deliberately small body for a broad delayed payoff. Burn can be cleansed before round end and ticks once, including the final round. Existing protection works per target. He does not burn hazards, buff himself, or buff support cards.

SQUABBLE doubles printed Hands only. Each card has three ordinary coaching tiers: BUDDY +1 self / -1 hit target / +1 self; FOLKS +1 self / +1 Fire ally / +1 self. Failed abilities do not grant upgrades.

Original transparent cutouts are preserved under artifacts/squabblemon/reference/buddy and reference/folks. Runtime WebP portraits retain the supplied alpha and have cache revisions. No generated replacement art was needed.

Verification covers both owners, Mythical selection, shields, mitigation, uncounterable tokens, burn expiration/final scoring, disabled abilities, upgrades, pack acquisition, and mobile/desktop card rendering. These are intentionally strong launch values; automated checks are not human PvP balance sessions.
