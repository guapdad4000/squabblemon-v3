# District reminders

Restored short rule descriptions directly beneath each district’s scores.

- Portrait phones: three lines in a fixed 36px text slot, inside a stable 108px district target.
- Wider screens: two lines in a fixed text slot.
- The complete district explainer keeps its themed artwork, location description, rules, strategy, and live status.
- Compact copy covers all 27 catalog locations. Original districts retain their short rules. Issued matches with different rules keep their own text rather than receiving a stale summary.
- No extra text container or background panel was added.

Validation: 16 responsive browser checks, 105 existing battle/location checks, and app typecheck passed. Browser coverage includes 390×844 and 320×640 phones, 1440×1000 desktop, 820×1180 and 1180×820 iPad orientations, and 844×390 phone landscape. Every catalog reminder fits within three lines on a short phone; every popup retains its full rule.

Open REVIEW.html for screenshots. Local implementation only; this task has not published the release mail or deployed the changes.
