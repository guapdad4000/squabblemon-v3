# Desktop battle wallpaper review

Portrait artwork supplied by story and activity encounters was being contained inside desktop boards. An extra layer stretched three location scenes over the full board, leaving visible background strips.

Known venue assets now use their matching portrait or landscape composition, with landscape artwork filling the battlefield uniformly. Custom story environments retain their issued artwork. The extra full-board location layer is removed; the three floating location illustrations remain beside the scores. Match artwork warmup selects the same orientation-specific asset.

Validation passed: 69 battle UI tests, production build, entry bundle budgets, 40 tablet venue cases, four rotation round trips, 15 desktop venue cases, seven real story cases, three phone/desktop regressions, and two fixture fallbacks. Browser checks cover loaded artwork, uniform scaling and full canvas coverage, retained fighters, controls, clocks, location nodes, and absence of runtime errors. Screenshots show the real Wifey encounter with the screenshot's locations in the local fixture at 1920×1080 and 3440×1440.
