# City After Dark: 11 new locations

27 current locations, plus 3 classic artwork entries. All new rules use the shared solo, story, CPU, replay, and online engine.

## UNDERGROUND RING

Only your strongest card here contributes Hands. Other cards still use abilities.

Commit a champion and use the rest as support. Tied champions count only once. Frozen cards cannot be champion.

## ROOFTOP GARDEN

At the start of rounds 2–6, every card here permanently gains +1 Hands.

Plant early and let your crew grow. The bonus travels with cards that move away; Frozen cards still grow.

## PAWN SHOP

Your first play here each round destroys your weakest other ally here and gains its current Hands before its ability.

Trade a small ally into a bigger threat. Frozen allies give 0; district bonuses are excluded. No other ally means no trade.

## PIRATE RADIO

While you have exactly 2 cards here, your side gains +3 district Hands in each other lane.

Hold a two-card broadcast crew. A third ally turns the signal off. The bonus can score in an empty lane.

## BLACKOUT BLOCK

Cards played here are Silenced before their abilities resolve. Moving in avoids the blackout.

Bring raw Hands or move your ability cards here. Silence stays after leaving until cleansed.

## FLOOD CHANNEL

At the start of round 4, all cards here move one lane right. Rightmost wraps left.

Build a crew for the flood, or wait until it passes. Locked cards stay. Movement triggers still apply; abilities do not repeat.

## CONSTRUCTION SITE

From round 4, neither side can play cards here. Cards can still move in or out.

Claim the site early, then use movement to reinforce or invade after the gates close.

## NIGHT MARKET

Your first play here each round draws the next card from your deck before its ability.

Find more options while you still have Motion to spend. An empty deck draws nothing; moving in does not draw.

## MIRROR ARCADE

Each card here scores its printed Motion cost instead of its current Hands. Frozen cards score 0.

Big costs become reliable scores. Buffs, penalties and SQUABBLE still matter when cards leave. Abilities still work.

## COMMUNITY KITCHEN

Your first play here each round gives all your cards in the other lanes +1 permanent Hands.

Spread your crew before serving. The meal resolves before the new card’s ability and never buffs cards in this lane.

## RUSH HOUR

The first play here each round, by either side, pushes every other card here one lane right before its ability.

Both crews share one traffic trigger per round. The new arrival stays; Locked cards resist. Rightmost wraps left.
