## Why the stronger engines work

Water has cheap pressure (Gator Boy), movement that also grows bodies (Cool Vibe YN), and repeatable Water growth from Gas Station Sushi Chef in hand. Plant/Mushroom combines automatic token supply from Demario with Plant hand growth and conversion into Luigion. Air can keep The Flight Plug in hand while deploying and moving its teammates. These engines generate value without waiting for the enemy to cooperate.

Fitness spans Normal, Water and Fire, so one elemental hand carrier does not grow its whole crew. Its ten-card recipe has few inexpensive movers, a three-district circuit, reactive recovery and enemy-destruction-only revenge. Cypher needs multiple movement events; the old Fire shell contains few movement enablers. Music has entrance payoffs but limited refunds/returns. Wave 7 is a mixed utility shell rather than a complete archetype. The Fire audit replaces GUAP and Folks; its score measures that diagnostic shell, not the full live GUAP deck.

Design rule: add redundant cheap starters, then bounded payoffs and answers. Do not give each archetype another unrestricted hand-growth carrier. Printed Motion/Hands below are starting hypotheses. Each successful entrance grants training once under the shared rules, not an additional unbounded bonus.

## Release allocation

Three eleven-card waves: first wave 3 Fitness + 3 Cypher + 2 Music + 1 entrance utility + 2 counterplay; second 3 + 3 + 2 + 2 + 1; third 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 3. Introduce starters before finishers. Prototype and test the first wave before committing all 33 names or mechanics. Concepts that overlap existing cards need a second identity pass before art production.

## Acceptance before implementation

Validate targeting, failure cases, caps shared by copies, return/echo interactions, silenced/frozen sources, full/locked districts, real damage accounting, both owners, online parity and replay. Compare several recipes and seeds; test with a movement-aware policy and humans. Bot scores from generic policies cannot establish parity for timing-heavy kits. Keep the catalog at 267 until characters have approved identity, art and working rules; this document does not consume collectible slots.
