# New character art

15 full-body transparent character cutouts generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Bold ink contours and cel shading referenced from the current Fitness Bro and Personal Trainer assets. Sunday Dinner characters also reference the user-provided lineup. Only fist emblems; no crowns or baked card text.

Open REVIEW.html to inspect all art against checkerboard, light and dark backgrounds. Original PNGs are in png/; optimized WebP files in webp/. PROMPTS.md stores the final prompts. manifest.json records source paths, dimensions, alpha bounds and sizes. All 15 originals have RGBA alpha ranging 0-255 with a transparent corner; all WebPs retain alpha. The review page decoded all 15 images successfully.

These assets are staged for future card implementation; they do not add live cards, change pull rates or overwrite existing art. Rejected realistic/background versions are excluded. Portable character props such as Grandma's chair and Grill Uncle's grill remain part of those silhouettes.
