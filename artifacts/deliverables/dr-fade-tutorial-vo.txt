OPTIONAL FIRST-SIGHTING MECHANIC LESSONS
========================================

These appear when a mechanic is first encountered. Each block combines the explanation and Coach’s call in display order.

32. mechanic-burn | Burn
Burn stacks remove that many Hands at round end, then the stacks clear. Cleanse it before round end or plan around the coming score loss.

33. mechanic-freeze | Frozen
A Frozen card adds 0 Hands and its ability cannot fire until it is cleansed. A frozen card still occupies its district, so a cleanse can bring it back online.

34. mechanic-cleanse | Cleanse
Cleanse removes Burn, Freeze, Silence, Weaken, and Lock from a card. Friendly buffs such as protection and Boost stay in place.

35. mechanic-protect | Protection
Protection blocks a targeted hostile effect. Some shields last for the round; Cover lasts until spent. Use protection before the rival targets your most valuable fighter.

36. mechanic-blocked | Blocked
The incoming targeted effect was stopped, so its intended change did not land. The protection that stopped it may now be spent; check the card status before the next attack.

37. mechanic-weaken | Weakened
A Weakened card keeps its Hands but cannot fire its ability. Cleanse restores the ability. The card can still help hold its district meanwhile.

38. mechanic-silence | Silenced
A Silenced card keeps its Hands but cannot fire its ability. Play for its raw Hands or cleanse it before you need the ability.

39. mechanic-movement | Movement
An ability moved a card to another district after it was already in play. Recheck both district scores after movement; one move can swing two districts.

40. mechanic-lock | Locked
A Locked card cannot be moved to another district until it is cleansed. Its Hands still count where it stands. Cleanse it if repositioning matters.

41. mechanic-boost | Boost
A Boosted card gains +1 Hands at every round end while Boost remains active. Early Boosts compound over several rounds, so answer them before they grow.

OPTIONAL DECK WORKSHOP — DR. FADE’S SUGGESTIONS
===============================================

Only suggestions for owned cards appear. These are optional expandable coaching tips, not mandatory steps in the tutorial.

47. suggestion-1 | Build an Earth tax crew
Landlord is an affordable opener: tax the first enemy arrival here each round, then pair him with Earth supports and wide finishers.

48. suggestion-2 | Echo movement value
The Wiz wants open districts: move a small ally with Dorothy, then let Oz repeat a successful entrance instead of stacking one lane.

49. suggestion-3 | Turn returns into tempo
Alice leaves the board once, then returns with a Hands boost and a cheaper redeployment. Cheshire turns each return into lasting board value.

50. suggestion-4 | Predict, cancel, protect
An actual Sherlock cancellation gives him +2 Hands and your weakest other character +2. Watson is a 2/3 who repairs up to 3 actual damage, Protects that ally or a fallback ally, and Protects Sherlock anywhere.

51. suggestion-5 | Finish with Fire
Build friendly characters across the map before GUAP: FINNAM! charges from the whole board and pressures every opposing district.

52. suggestion-6 | Chain Poison entries
Bottle Girl rewards a later play and discounts your next Poison character. Follow with entry punishment from Cologne Criminal or Nail Tech.

53. suggestion-7 | Support first, inmates wide
Play a real support card first, then follow with 2/3 Crafty for +2 Hands. Spread inmates so Boyfriend can give +2 locally and +1 across districts.

54. suggestion-8 | Set up the Luigion jump
The 2/2 Demario opens with one local Mushroom. Normal or Powered Luigion consumes it once for +2; SQUABBLE is optional for the powered jump.

55. suggestion-9 | Trigger Dark control
Use Silence and Weaken enablers such as Closet Nerd, BUDDY, and Red Pill. Each round, the first new debuff activates Counter and Gamer’s Dark payoff.
