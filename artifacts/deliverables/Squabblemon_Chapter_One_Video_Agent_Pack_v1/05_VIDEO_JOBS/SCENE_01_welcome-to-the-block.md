## Staging

Late afternoon. Somebody has cable-tied a red carpet to the corner-store fence. Half the OPEN ENTRY sign is covered by Blue's jacket; underneath it, Cornball has taped a handwritten VIP sign to the vending machine. He is addressing the machine through a karaoke microphone that is absolutely not plugged in. The player walks up with a deck. Blue puts on sunglasses to inspect them, then takes them off because the price sticker is still on one lens.

## Shot plan

### ch01_s01_pre_sh01

Wide push-in. Reveal dead karaoke mic and vending-machine press conference.

**Cornball:** This machine stole my three dollars. I am outside its place of business demanding accountability.

### ch01_s01_pre_sh02

Blue/Cornball two-shot. Blue inspects the newcomer; Cornball indicts the covered OPEN sign.

**Ganger Blue:** Who you with? And don't say yourself. Everybody bringing themselves. That's the problem.

**Ganger Blue:** No Blue. No Red. You just showed up unseasoned?

**Cornball:** The sign says OPEN, Blue. You can't put a jacket over a vowel and start a private school.

Alternating medium and reaction. Challenge terms, district rule, bass-in-the-car roast. Finish on deck/table handoff.

**Ganger Blue:** Deck. Table. Now. Before I charge your commentator a vendor fee.

### ch01_s01_post_sh04

Medium reaction, then prop insert. Blue swallows the loss; jacket yanks off sign; drink drops halfway and stops.

**Ganger Blue:** Come through Blue Side if you want more than a name on this sheet.

**Ganger Blue:** And don't write CHAMPION in the nickname box. I know how y'all get.

## Exit

Blue snatches his jacket off the sign so hard the VIP sign falls off the machine. Cornball catches it and wears it as a badge. The drink drops halfway, stops, and stays there. Cornball turns his dead microphone toward it for a response.
