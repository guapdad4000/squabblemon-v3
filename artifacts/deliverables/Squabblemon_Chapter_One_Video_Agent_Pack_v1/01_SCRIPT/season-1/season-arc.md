# Season One — Block Party

Revised 2026-09-08. Canon: [story bible](../STORY_BIBLE.md). Eight chapters, approximately six weeks. Chapter 1 is scripted; Chapters 2–8 are outlines.

## Act I — Who gets to play?

### 1. Block Party
The newcomer enters an Open Blue and Red treat as private competition. Defeating both earns an independent place on the contender list. Snitch turns the climb into content for an unnamed challenger. At the rooftop final, Cracked Head appears. Blue recognizes the brother he believed dead; Red's lack of surprise becomes a clue. The player wins the Crown and scheduling rights. Baby Momma demands a private conversation. Red admits he has something she should hear first.

### 2. Red Side Tapes
Baby Momma seeks the original message Red concealed. The player keeps the Red Side qualifier open while competing claims about footage threaten to close it. Baby Momma is the final opponent because she contests control of the next public event; custody and truth are never match prizes. The message proves Cracked Head survived the memorial and knew about their child. Red admits money felt easier than honesty. Baby Momma takes the original to OG Uncle. He answers: “I told him to let you grieve.”

## Act II — Who paid for the secret?

### 3. Blue Side Blues
Three compact encounters around a disrupted Blue Side event. Wifey keeps it operating while Blue ignores his father's calls. The player's wins preserve open slots Blue wanted canceled. Wifey insists he visit OG Uncle himself. Blue learns his father is seriously ill and maintained the death story, then leaves before hearing why. Wifey stays and asks whether OG Uncle knows who gave Snitch the address.

### 4. Side Show
Snitch releases an edited recording that makes Red look responsible for everything. Scammer sells supposed alternate cuts. The player competes through a sideshow event as the feud draws attention from its actual participants. Dates, continuous audio, and the original message establish authenticity; winning cannot. After losing an agreed interview challenge, Snitch goes on record. Red insists on publishing the full cut despite his own exposure. Snitch names Blue as the address source. Blue arrives at OG Uncle's door without a deck.

### 5. Old Heads Know
An Old Heads event brings the family into one courtyard. OG Uncle's two-phase final tests patience and recovery; he plays seated, and his illness is never a combat gimmick. Afterward he explains the rescue and owns the false memorial. Blue admits wanting his brother embarrassed and displaced. Cracked Head hears both truths. Wifey admits to Baby Momma that she knew about the address and stayed silent. Baby Momma refuses their argument about whose betrayal was worse. Cracked Head demands a public title challenge against Blue.

## Act III — What is a crown worth?

### 6. The Function
Three compact matches at Bottle Girl and Promoter's fundraiser. The player defends open entry. Crew crossings happen through setup jobs, games, and a meal, not a roll call of cameos. Cracked Head treats the next challenge as his restoration ceremony. Baby Momma says an appearance is not parenting. Blue signs equal-access terms for the final series. Wifey sees him take the contender list back after the room empties. Cracked Head's name is crossed out.

### 7. Return of the Block
Cracked Head returns to formal competition; everyone already knows he is alive. The altered list threatens the tournament. The player defends their place while Blue calls exclusion protection. Blue finally restores the list publicly and accepts disqualification. His restitution costs his title shot. Cracked Head earns his own final place and acknowledges the player as his rightful opponent. Their Chapter 7 encounter is a published exhibition/seeding match, so defeat does not eliminate him from Chapter 8. A rooftop inspection interrupts the celebration: the owner has invited a buyer.

### 8. The Crown
The concluding Open crosses three venues before its rooftop final. “The Block” is the event name, not a surprise sentient opponent. Cracked Head is the three-phase final rival. Blue volunteers on setup; Red runs the complete feed; Snitch has to credit its source. The player wins. Cracked Head accepts shared scheduling instead of inherited authority. At Church Auntie's meal he agrees to a specific parenting commitment the next morning, on Baby Momma's terms. OG Uncle lives to sit with both sons. The owner confirms a sale agreement with Techbro Rich: the Crown secures the Open's rules, not the building.
