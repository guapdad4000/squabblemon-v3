# Chapter One: Block Party — full read-through

Heightened comedy-drama revision · 2026-09-08 · 8 scenes · [Story bible](../../STORY_BIBLE.md)

The Open has barely started and everybody's already acting like somebody owes them royalties. A newcomer wants a place to play. Blue wants jurisdiction over folding chairs. Red wants the whole recording, except the one he has been hiding. Then a supposedly dead champion shows up with entrance music—and the woman he never called shows up without any.

The player is unvoiced. Six required battles lead to the ceremony; the alley match is optional. Stage directions are not dialogue choices. This screenplay is written but not yet loaded into the game.

## 1. Welcome to the Block

*corner store court.* Late afternoon. Somebody has cable-tied a red carpet to the corner-store fence. Half the OPEN ENTRY sign is covered by Blue's jacket; underneath it, Cornball has taped a handwritten VIP sign to the vending machine. He is addressing the machine through a karaoke microphone that is absolutely not plugged in. The player walks up with a deck. Blue puts on sunglasses to inspect them, then takes them off because the price sticker is still on one lens.

**Cornball:** This machine stole my three dollars. I am outside its place of business demanding accountability.

**Ganger Blue:** Who you with? And don't say yourself. Everybody bringing themselves. That's the problem.

**Ganger Blue:** No Blue. No Red. You just showed up unseasoned?

**Cornball:** The sign says OPEN, Blue. You can't put a jacket over a vowel and start a private school.

**Ganger Blue:** Beat me, you get a slot. Lose, get back in line. This ain't your cousin's mixtape release. Everybody don't get a turn.

**Ganger Blue:** Two districts wins it. Don't make me say it twice.

**Cornball:** He said it eleven times in the car. We missed the exit because he wanted bass under it.

**Ganger Blue:** Deck. Table. Now. Before I charge your commentator a vendor fee.

*BATTLE — guided. The player must win to continue this scene's route. Existing game rules and rewards apply.*

**Ganger Blue:** Okay. You got a slot. Don't be standing there looking like you just bought me.

**Cornball:** First day outside and already somebody's supervisor. That's nasty work.

**Ganger Blue:** Come through Blue Side if you want more than a name on this sheet.

**Ganger Blue:** And don't write CHAMPION in the nickname box. I know how y'all get.

*Blue snatches his jacket off the sign so hard the VIP sign falls off the machine. Cornball catches it and wears it as a badge. The drink drops halfway, stops, and stays there. Cornball turns his dead microphone toward it for a response.*

## 2. Blue Side Pressure

*harbor skyline court.* Harbor lights rise behind a borrowed throne made from two folding chairs and a gold graduation sash. Blue is about to sit when Wifey removes the sash, folds one chair, and hands him an extension cable. A box labeled BLUE SIDE EXECUTIVE OPERATIONS contains paper plates and a single oven mitt. The player arrives while Blue tries to kick the box under the table.

**Ganger Blue:** Everybody want a seat at my table. Nobody ask who built the empire.

**Wifey:** Your empire is plugged into Nail Tech's bathroom. Don't nobody use that hair dryer.

**Ganger Blue:** I kept these lights on!

**Wifey:** WE kept them on. You brought a speaker and asked if we had ice.

**Ganger Blue:** Winner gets the Crown. Runs the next open-play schedule. That's real power.

**Wifey:** You pick who plays at seven. You are not the mayor of Electricity.

**Ganger Blue:** You want slots for your little independent situation? Beat me right here. In front of my taxpayers.

*BATTLE — standard. The player must win to continue this scene's route. Existing game rules and rewards apply.*

**Ganger Blue:** Fine. Your crew gets in. No colors. I said what I said.

**Wifey:** And I wrote what you said. In ink. We know how your memory acts after a loss.

**Ganger Blue:** Red's over there filming everything. Go be somebody else's emergency.

**Wifey:** Take the big entrance. He been practicing his disappointed face since lunch.

*Blue finally sits. The folding chair gives one warning creak. Wifey points at the cable box without looking. He stands back up and carries it. One court light flickers as a distant hair dryer starts; everybody freezes until it stops.*

## 3. Receipts on Camera

*red fence night court.* Red's court looks like a community-access courtroom. A phone sits beneath a cardboard rain hood labeled OFFICIAL MEDIA. Snitch has clipped a ring light onto the maintenance barrier and is trying to get a flattering angle on an argument that has not happened yet. Red places a kitchen timer beside the deck. Its ticking is much louder than necessary.

**Ganger Red:** I watched your match. Then I watched Blue explain your match. That's two different genres.

**Snitch:** His explanation got more views. Facts need better lighting.

**Ganger Red:** Film both sides of the table. Last time your thumb had more screen time than the winner.

**Ganger Red:** Middle district closes in round two. Both of us. That orange barrier ain't an aesthetic.

**Snitch:** Damn. You doing the safety briefing before the beatdown?

**Ganger Red:** I'm removing your excuses in alphabetical order.

**Ganger Red:** Win and your name goes forward. Whole match stays up. No cousins in the comments demanding a recount.

*BATTLE — rule-twist. The player must win to continue this scene's route. Existing game rules and rewards apply.*

**Snitch:** I got the PERFECT title. LOCAL MAN HUMILIATED IN FRONT OF HIS OWN PHONE.

**Ganger Red:** Keep the beginning. They earned the win. You didn't discover it.

**Ganger Red:** Your name goes forward. One more Red Side test. Don't turn a good afternoon into a personality.

**Snitch:** Too late. Blue's already posted a paragraph with no punctuation.

*Red writes down his own loss with painful precision. Snitch rotates the ring light toward him. Red rotates it straight back toward the table without looking up. The kitchen timer rings. Nobody ordered food.*

## 4. Red Side Retaliation

*red fence night court.* Red has watched his loss back three times, once in slow motion. Snitch's thumbnail has given him a cartoon tear, visible briefly on the face-up phone before Red flips it over. Blue arrives carrying a paper plate as if he has come only to spectate. A folded memorial program slips from Red's notebook: Cracked Head's portrait, gold clouds, and an unnecessary white dove. Blue's smile goes.

**Ganger Red:** My auntie called. Said if I lose again, don't bring the Red Side banner to her cookout.

**Ganger Blue:** That's family accountability. Beautiful to see.

**Ganger Red:** You lost twice and called it community outreach. Put that plate down.

**Ganger Blue:** Hold up. Why you got that program?

**Ganger Red:** It was in the notebook.

**Ganger Blue:** That's my brother, Red. Four years. You carrying his memorial next to a tournament bracket?

**Ganger Red:** Cracked Head taught us this table. I kept it. That's all I'm saying.

**Ganger Red:** One more match. Win, I sign your final entry. Save some Motion. I'm not coming gentle in round four.

*BATTLE — standard. The player must win to continue this scene's route. Existing game rules and rewards apply.*

**Ganger Red:** You won. I'll sign. Somebody tell my auntie I'm still bringing the potato salad.

**Ganger Blue:** Don't put that program on the stream.

**Ganger Red:** I wasn't going to.

**Ganger Blue:** Yeah. You got a whole museum of shit you weren't going to.

**Ganger Red:** Snitch has the contender sheet up the hill. Get there before he puts your entry behind a subscription.

*Red signs the entry. Blue leaves the untouched plate on the table and walks off. Red folds the memorial slowly enough to conceal a smaller recording sleeve behind it. Hold that action without a joke. He knows more than he is saying.*

## 5. Side Alley Challenge — optional

*corner store court.* An alley table rests on a courier bag and a folded eviction flyer someone has crossed out and rewritten as EVENT FLYER. Alley Runner is sorting deliveries while Cornball has returned to the vending machine with a clipboard. He has listed himself as lead investigator, key witness, and refreshments coordinator. This optional scene can happen any time after Blue Side Pressure; no dialogue assumes whether the Crown has been won.

**Alley Runner:** Main route's over there. This table's optional. I know. A choice. We don't get many of those around here.

**Cornball:** I've opened a case against this machine. Three dollars missing. Suspect refuses to blink.

**Alley Runner:** That's the display window. Your drink's behind the flap.

**Cornball:** Then why they got it behind glass like a damn museum piece?

**Alley Runner:** Win here, you get a cardback tag. No crew tax. No uncle you gotta impress.

**Alley Runner:** Watch the whole table. I move around. Unlike your boy's investigation.

*BATTLE — standard. The player must win to continue this scene's route. Existing game rules and rewards apply.*

**Alley Runner:** Tag's yours. If anybody asks what side you're on, show 'em the back of the card.

**Cornball:** Got my drink. Room temperature. That's how they serve revenge.

**Alley Runner:** Watch Snitch. He waits till people run out of answers, then starts acting like he invented the question.

**Cornball:** He charged me to correct my own birthday post.

**Alley Runner:** Keep something for the late rounds. Let him interview the loss.

*Cornball takes a sip, studies the label, and discovers he has spent the evening fighting for unsweetened sparkling water. Alley Runner quietly passes him a packet of sugar from the courier bag. Cornball regards it as an insult and pockets it anyway.*

## 6. Snitch at the Corner

*civic hill climb.* Snitch has built a media desk on the hill from a folding table and an unopened air-fryer box. A homemade sign reads EXCLUSIVE, with the price crossed out twice and increased. A second phone buzzes; he hides it under the contender sheet. Red arrives as Snitch applies lip balm using the black screen of the player's match replay.

**Snitch:** Look who survived both sides. Independent crew, undefeated attitude. Your documentary just got a second episode.

**Ganger Red:** Post the contender sheet. You are not the Department of Outside.

**Snitch:** One qualifier. Same as everybody. This is a professional operation.

**Ganger Red:** You're sitting on an air fryer. Who asked you for the footage?

**Snitch:** A returning customer. Very emotional demographic.

**Snitch:** I watched your matches. Round four, I get active. That's when the subscription renews.

**Ganger Red:** Two extra Motion. Keep some for it. The ring light doesn't make him mysterious.

**Snitch:** Could you stop reading my damn business model out loud?

*BATTLE — mini-boss. The player must win to continue this scene's route. Existing game rules and rewards apply.*

**Snitch:** Your slot's up. Don't tag me in the celebration. I'm rebranding.

**Ganger Red:** Who is the other finalist?

**Snitch:** They paid for an entrance. I'm contractually obligated to let this get messy.

**Ganger Red:** You made a contract?

**Snitch:** Voice note and a deposit. That's a contract with feelings.

*A gospel-organ flourish blasts from upstairs, clips into static, and stops halfway through a chord. Heavy footsteps follow. Red looks up and goes still. Snitch snatches both phones off the air fryer. Whoever arranged the entrance has arrived.*

## 7. Cracked Head Takes the Block

*crown rooftop court.* Rooftop final. Snitch has taped gold gift wrap down as a red carpet; one corner is stuck to his shoe. The door swings open. Cracked Head stands in a cloud of smoke from the overworked fish-fry station downstairs, not a supernatural effect. Snitch jabs a soundboard. A huge organ chord lands, followed by a cheerful BATTERY LOW announcement. Blue reaches the landing, sees his brother, and stops so abruptly that Red nearly walks into him. Hold on his face. The joke has arrived at a real wound.

**Ganger Blue:** Oh, hell no. Not in the hoodie we buried on the flyer.

**Cracked Head:** What's good, little brother?

**Ganger Blue:** WHAT'S GOOD? I put your face on shirts! I argued with the printer about your damn hairline!

**Cracked Head:** Blue. I'm standing right here.

**Ganger Blue:** That's the problem! We had a memorial! There was a slideshow! You smiled in transition number SIX!

**Ganger Red:** Snitch. Camera down. Right now.

**Snitch:** I'm live on two phones. I don't even know which one to disappoint first.

**Cracked Head:** Let it run. Everybody got something to say about me. They can say it while I'm here.

**Ganger Blue:** Four years, no call. But you found the championship? Your GPS don't work for family?

**Cracked Head:** I came back for what's mine.

**Ganger Red:** That chair ain't yours. They earned it. You want the Crown, play the finalist.

**Cracked Head:** All right, new face. Pull up. Let's see if the block got better or just louder.

*BATTLE — boss. The player must win to continue this scene's route. Existing game rules and rewards apply.*

**Cracked Head:** You got me. Crown's yours. Don't make me regret saying it in front of all these phones.

**Ganger Blue:** Look at that. He can acknowledge a stranger. Somebody get this miracle on the church calendar.

**Cracked Head:** Blue, give me a damn minute.

**Ganger Blue:** You had four years. That's a lot of damn minutes.

**Ganger Red:** The result stands. Family meeting is a different table.

**Cracked Head:** I'll be back at this one.

**Ganger Red:** Then sign up. The ghost discount expired.

*Cracked Head offers Blue a hand. Blue looks at it as if it has arrived four years late with no tracking number, then walks past him. Snitch's soundboard accidentally plays APPLAUSE. Red unplugs it. Cracked Head moves his deck out of the winner's space.*

## 8. Block Crowned

*crown rooftop court.* Snitch produces a plastic Crown from a velvet cushion suspiciously shaped like a neck pillow. Wifey arrives with the actual scheduling board. Cornball distributes paper cups and explains to nobody that the cup is complimentary but his time is not. Baby Momma reaches the stairs carrying a handbag large enough to contain either paperwork or a small home appliance. She disconnects the ring light before speaking. Keep the child off-screen. No optional-alley completion is assumed.



**Ganger Red:** We got a winner. Crown, schedule, seat for your crew. Please don't start appointing cousins to positions.

**Wifey:** Open play means everybody. I'm laminating this. Y'all respect plastic more than a promise.

**Ganger Blue:** I'll bring the lights. Don't make a whole announcement about it.

**Cornball:** I'll bring drinks. Bought from a human. With witnesses.

**Snitch:** Everybody squeeze in. Winner in the middle, resurrected gentleman slightly to the—

**Baby Momma:** Unplug whatever is making you comfortable.

**Snitch:** We're just doing a little celebration—

**Baby Momma:** I see. Four years dead and his ass somehow found VIP.

**Cracked Head:** I was coming to see you.

**Baby Momma:** When? After the press tour? My address ain't changed. Even the pizza man knows when I'm upset.

**Cracked Head:** Can we do this somewhere private?

**Baby Momma:** NOW you believe in privacy. Came through that door with smoke and a church organ like you got a season finale.

**Ganger Red:** Before y'all go. I got something she needs to hear.

**Baby Momma:** Red. That envelope better be an apology with direct deposit.

**Ganger Red:** It's a recording. From him. Four years ago.

**Baby Momma:** Four years? Oh, put the chairs back. Apparently BOTH of y'all got a presentation.

*Baby Momma takes the recording sleeve from Red and pulls out the nearest chair. Cracked Head starts to sit beside her. She slides it toward Red instead. Wifey silently sets down two unopened bottles of water like this meeting has been scheduled for months. Cornball retreats with the cups. Snitch lifts one phone an inch; Baby Momma looks at him and it goes face down. Cut before the recording plays. Leave the Crown crooked on its cushion beside the player's deck.*
