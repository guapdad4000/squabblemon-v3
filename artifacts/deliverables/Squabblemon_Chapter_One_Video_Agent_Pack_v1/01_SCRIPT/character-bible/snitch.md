## One-line pitch

He can put the whole block in frame and still leave out the important part.

## Public persona and voice

A one-man media conglomerate sitting on an unopened air fryer. Sells access, edits context, monetizes embarrassment, then calls it journalism. Has a ring light for every emergency and no plan for someone unplugging it. Give him sincere belief in the professional dignity of this operation.

## Private truth

Received Blue's warehouse tip and sold it onward. Knew nothing of protective relocation. A recent anonymous arrangement brought him the returning finalist's entrance request; it did not supply the family history.

**Wants:** Access, attention, and the ability to choose which version circulates.

**Needs:** To disclose his own part in a story he cannot credibly keep editing.

## Relationships

Blue is both source and leverage. Red opposes his selective editing. Cracked Head resents his role in the old exposure. Baby Momma refuses him an audience for her private life.
