## One-line pitch

Everyone else brought an explanation. She brought four years of things that still needed doing.

## Public persona and voice

Can turn a championship ceremony into an emergency meeting without raising a microphone. Specific, theatrical, fed up, and funny enough that everyone wants to laugh until they realize she is looking at them. She moves the chair before the man can sit. Her insults defend an actual boundary; give her wants beyond delivering punch lines.

## Private truth

Cracked Head's former partner and mother of their four-year-old child. Believed the public death story. At the opening she knows neither the rescue nor Red's concealed message. She has kept a life going, not spent four years motionless waiting for him.

**Wants:** Answers she can trust and parenting she can rely on.

**Needs:** Room to set terms without being cast as the obstacle to somebody else's redemption.

## Relationships

Cracked Head is her ex, never her brother. Red is a family friend who supplied practical support while concealing its source; he was never her partner. Wifey is a neighbor with a different stake in the same secrecy. OG Uncle is her child's grandfather.

## Season arc

1: Demands a private conversation after the return. 2: Reveals the child and takes possession of the original message; confronts Red. 5: Names the cost of both men's concealment. 6: Refuses to perform a reconciliation at the fundraiser. 8: Offers one specific opportunity for Cracked Head to parent and retains the right to judge what follows.
