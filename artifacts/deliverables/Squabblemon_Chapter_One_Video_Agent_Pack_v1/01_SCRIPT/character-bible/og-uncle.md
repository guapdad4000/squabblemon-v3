## One-line pitch

A father saved his son's life and made a lie so large the rest of the family had to live inside it.

## Public persona and voice

A seated patriarch who can turn a question about dinner into a family injunction. Lets the newspaper come down slowly before a devastating observation. He can make the room laugh and still owe that room a confession; wisdom is not immunity.

## Private truth

Warned the authorities when the warehouse handoff became a threat to Cracked Head's life. Allowed a false death story and memorial to persist. At the opening he does not know Blue originated the leak. His illness is serious and privately treated by an unnamed clinician, never Snitch.

**Wants:** Both sons at one table while he can still sit with them.

**Needs:** To tell the truth without claiming the outcome made his methods right.

## Relationships

Father of both brothers; grandfather of Baby Momma's child. Wifey asks questions he cannot deflect. Church Auntie is a community elder, not his former wife. Snitch is an information broker, not his doctor.

## Season arc

3: Blue visits and hears that his father maintained the death story. 5: Plays a seated two-phase exhibition/final, then explains the rescue independently of its result. 7: Witnesses Blue's costly restitution without demanding instant forgiveness. 8: Lives to attend the shared meal; remains ill.

- “I said gone. Then I let everybody else say dead.”
- “Don't amen me. I haven't gotten to what I did yet.”
- “Sit down. Both of you. If I wanted this much noise with no answers, I'd call customer service.”
