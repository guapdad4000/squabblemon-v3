## One-line pitch

A man who mistakes keeping the lights on for owning the switch.

## Public persona and voice

A self-appointed neighborhood executive with a folding-chair throne and somebody else's extension cord. Loud confidence, quick commands, elaborate explanations for plain losses. His wife can reduce the whole speech to an unpaid chore. Let his grief arrive at the same volume as his bragging, then leave him nowhere to hide.

## Private truth

He gave Snitch the warehouse address to embarrass and displace Cracked Head. He knew the risk, did not intend a death, and believed for four years that his brother had died. He does not know about OG Uncle's rescue before the return.

**Wants:** Recognition as the brother who stayed.

**Needs:** To stop charging everyone else for the guilt he will not name.

## Relationships

Cracked Head is his elder brother; OG Uncle is their father; Wifey is his wife. Red is a lifelong friend and rival. Snitch knows the original tip. Wifey knows Blue supplied the address, but not the rescue.

## Season arc

1: Loses both required matches; gives the newcomer their earned place; is shocked by his brother's return. 3: Visits OG Uncle but leaves before hearing the whole account. 4: Is named as the source. 5: Confesses his motive. 6: Signs equal-access terms, then alters the list. 7: Restores it and accepts disqualification. 8: Works setup and teaches a newcomer with patience.
