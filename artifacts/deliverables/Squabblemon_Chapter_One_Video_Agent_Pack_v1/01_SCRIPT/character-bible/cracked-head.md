## One-line pitch

A missing champion comes home expecting a welcome that only an accountable father and brother could earn.

## Public persona and voice

Enters like the reunion special has been waiting for him. Slow declarations, territorial confidence, the assumption that every room kept his chair. He will stand in fish-fry smoke and let it look intentional. His grand return collapses when Blue names what four years actually felt like.

## Private truth

Survived the exposed warehouse handoff and entered fictional protective relocation after cooperating against its organizers. OG Uncle kept the false death story alive. Cracked Head later learned about his child, sent one message through Red, and failed to sustain contact. He does not know Blue originated the leak.

**Wants:** His place at the head of the table.

**Needs:** To show up consistently at tables where he cannot dictate the terms.

## Relationships

Blue is his younger brother; OG Uncle their father; Red his former right hand. Baby Momma is his former partner and the mother of their four-year-old child. They are not siblings. Snitch sold the location onward.

## Season arc

1: Publicly returns, loses to the player, and honors the result. 2: Must answer for the concealed message and years of silence. 5: Learns Blue's motive and OG Uncle's choices. 6: Demands Blue's displacement. 7: Accepts equal-entry terms and qualifies for the final. 8: Loses the final, keeps a concrete parenting commitment, and begins rebuilding trust.

His return occurs in Chapter 1. Chapter 7 is a return to competition, never a second survival reveal. No ghost, flashback battle, second pregnancy, or sale of a rooftop he does not own. Protection explains the departure, not every subsequent choice.
