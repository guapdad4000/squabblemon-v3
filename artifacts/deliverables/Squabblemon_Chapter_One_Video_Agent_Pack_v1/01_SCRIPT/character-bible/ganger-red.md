## One-line pitch

A keeper of records who preserved everything except the courage to deliver it.

## Public persona and voice

Treats a card table like a courtroom and corrects people with surgical irritation. Has an auntie who can ban his banner from the cookout. He will roast Blue, acknowledge a loss, and insist on every second of a recording—while hiding the most important one himself. His notebook is organized; his conscience is not.

## Private truth

Knew Cracked Head survived and carried an early message about Baby Momma and the child. Supplied practical support while hiding the message and its source. Did not know the precise return date or who exposed the handoff.

**Wants:** To hold Red Side together without forcing a reckoning.

**Needs:** To deliver the truth even when the full record ruins his preferred account of himself.

## Relationships

Lifelong friend of both brothers and Cracked Head's former right hand. Baby Momma is not his ex. He distrusts Snitch's edits while practicing his own form of omission.

## Season arc

1: Loses both matches, honors both results, and gives away prior knowledge by not reacting like Blue. Offers the recording to Baby Momma. 2: Delivers it privately and admits concealment. 4: Demands the full recording despite his own exposure. 8: Runs an accountable public feed.
