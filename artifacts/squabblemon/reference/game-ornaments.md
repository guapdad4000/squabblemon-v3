## Usage

- `public/assets/ui/fight-emblem.webp`: training, story battles, Squabble, result emblem.
- `public/assets/ui/motion-bolt.webp`: player/rival Motion and the Motion training activity.
- `public/assets/ui/action-gold.webp`: primary live-text buttons across battle and studio destinations.
- Existing illustrated deck, map, phone, and championship-chain props are reused.
- Utility marks and reward symbols remain scalable vectors, stamped on the existing medallion art.
- Labels remain accessible HTML. Decorative art is hidden from assistive technology.
- Hover/press/selection effects are finite and respect system and in-game reduced-motion settings.
