# Character art direction

User direction, September 17, 2026: characters should be a lot skinnier, with more dynamic poses from here on out.

- Default to lean, narrow builds: slender limbs, narrower shoulders and waist, and light athletic definition. Keep clothes readable without using oversized bulk to fill the frame.
- Choose a distinctive action for each character: lunging, slipping a strike, spinning, sliding, reaching through a turn, or a dance freeze. Use bent joints, a clear weight shift, torso rotation, and an asymmetric silhouette.
- Show the full character, hands, feet, and defining props. Keep enough transparent margin that a card or battle effect does not crop them.
- Maintain the established polished anime fighting-game linework and cel shading. Use genuinely transparent alpha and local aura details.
- Preserve supplied finished portraits, likeness details, and intentional aura unless the user asks to change them.

Reusable prompt addition: "Much leaner, skinnier build with slender limbs and a narrow torso. A distinctive dynamic action pose with a strong diagonal line of action, torso twist, bent joints, and convincing weight shift. Full body and all props visible."
