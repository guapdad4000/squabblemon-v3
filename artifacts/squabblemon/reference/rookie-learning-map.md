# Rookie Road learning map

Authority: the shared engine and the server-issued `rookie-road-v2` encounter, not recorded voice clips or a static screenshot. The guided match is four rounds, with no turn clock, a small rival opening hand and limited rival Motion. Normal matches default to six rounds, but story rules and Blockbusters can change the length. The server replays the exact taught moves before accepting tutorial completion.

| When | What the new player must be able to explain | Where it is taught |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Safehouse and gang setup | The collection is every owned card; a legal gang has ten different owned cards. Its cover is a visual identity, not a combat bonus. Cards 1–5 open in hand; later slots draw one each round while available. Motion cost, Base Hands and effect text are different things. A swap changes which draw is available when. | Safehouse tour, deck workbench, card details, swap review |
| Before first move | Claim at least two of three districts **at the finish**. Top is Rival, bottom is You; each district has its own score and tied districts belong to neither side. The coached encounter is shorter and timer-free; regular encounters may change clocks, modifiers or length. | Pre-fight goal brief, battle claim counter and district mats |
| A complete turn | Select the highlighted affordable card, read the district rule and legal lane, inspect without committing, then play. Preview estimates known board effects but cannot guarantee the rival response. A normal turn may play several affordable cards; End Turn passes to the rival. Motion refills to the next round number plus at most one unspent, capped at nine. | Live coaching, selection preview, in-match quick rules |
| Effects and response | Before/after Hands, Motion and all district scores are facts from event snapshots, not narrative guesses. Some effects change no score immediately; status and protection can defer or prevent changes. Reopen history or replay an event without altering the live match. SQUABBLE adds Base Hands once per match before other modifiers. | Event recap, first-occurrence mechanic lesson, history/replay, round recap |
| At the result | Claims, not the sum of all Hands, determine win/draw/loss; identify a contested district to improve next time. The taught opponent and one-card sequence are not every fight. | Final-board debrief, optional five-question teach-back |
| After the result | Starter cards and the saved gang persist. Practice is optional and does not claim the welcome reward; Chapter One follows claiming it. Clout funds purchases and coaching, Profile XP grows account level, Street Rep tracks account progress, character XP unlocks card coaching, pack tickets pay for packs and Style Shards are for cosmetics. | Handoff, reward primer, Field Guide |

Optional reference (never a mandatory four-round detour): support cards, Blockbusters, statuses/cleanse/protection, special districts and story modifiers, card levels/coaching, packs/odds, missions, other modes and online clocks. Rules stay in an in-match modal; the full Field Guide is reachable outside a match.

## Fresh-player teach-back

Observe a newcomer using a fresh account on a phone and desktop. Ask **before revealing** each optional answer: (1) What decides the winner? (2) When can you play another card and when does the rival respond? (3) What changed the last district score, and how would you inspect it? (4) Why did this board end in a win, draw or loss? (5) What will you do next, and which rewards are already saved? Record their own words and any incorrect or uncertain answer. A click-only scripted run is not a substitute for this observation; the in-result self-check makes the same five questions available without blocking progress.
