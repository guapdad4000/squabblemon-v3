## dr-fade-referee.webp

Preserve his Black male face, swept-back brown locs, glasses, rolled white sleeves, green tie, black vest and orange shoulder straps, muscular build and existing richly inked comic game art style. Change the pose: waist-up facing slightly right, friendly authoritative expression, one hand with a whistle near chest, other points toward the lower right where menu options will appear. No punching bag, no lightning, no text, no white sticker outline. Genuine transparent background. Entire head and arms within frame, clear cutout silhouette. Polished production game sprite.
