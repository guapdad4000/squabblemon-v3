# Homies / Friendly Fades — painted device handoff

The existing Fighter ID is a file-like container with a header, three horizontal tabs, and a scrolling panel; it is not currently an illustrated communications object. The Friendly Fade lobby opens with a rooftop venue, a large typographic two-fighter poster and ticket before the room setup controls. For Homies, place the illustrated device *inside the existing tab shell* as the dominant surface, and let the responsive invitation hub lead with actual contacts, crew and action rather than another poster. Do not replace the ranked venue or active battle art.

The existing sticker-phone prop is a charming tilted, closed-screen sticker illustration, so its perspective and baked chat bubbles cannot serve as an HTML aperture. The flagship Fadecade is an upright neon arcade marquee with a smaller dark aperture and a deliberately different high-saturation palette. The new front elevations keep the hand-inked, scratched, cream/ochre/forest/rust identity of navigation-painted profile art while providing large uninterrupted display cutouts. The tablet is separately composed landscape hardware, not a rotated phone. The generated devices include a cream crown sticker, ochre tape, green enamel and controlled rust wear confined to the rails. Do not put live controls over the bezel or the ink border.

Use `public/assets/homies/squabble-phone.webp` on portrait layouts and `squabble-tablet.webp` on landscape layouts, changing only the frame on orientation; do not remount the contact form or discard pending selection. `safe-control-bounds.json` describes the exact whole-image coordinates. The screen apertures are genuinely alpha-zero (not painted black). Place an HTML screen surface *behind* the image and place accessible scrolling controls in front within `safeControls`, or position controls over a screen-colored backing layer; reserve the final row and CTA from bezel overlap. Never cover or distort the frame with `object-fit: cover`. At small widths the art can scale uniformly, but 44px touch targets remain non-negotiable, so the screen may need the available viewport width or a thoughtfully scrollable overall shell. The frame is decorative; the HTML tab and data remain the source of truth. Missing images should leave a usable rectangular HTML screen and controls.

The retained 1024-square PNG generation masters were made with Replit's platform `generateImage` callback. Replit's [official Agent Image Generation documentation](https://docs.replit.com/features/agent/image-generation) (checked 2026-09-28) identifies Google Imagen 4 as the platform image-generation model. The callback itself exposes no per-job model identifier/version or model selection; we do not claim a verified per-job model ID. The masters have transparent outside pixels but intentionally painted solid displays, since model-generated rectangular transparency is unreliable. `optimize.mjs` crops/pads, sizes and precisely opens the rectangular displays in alpha before WebP optimization. It requires ImageMagick `magick` in PATH; rerun it at repository root with `node artifacts/squabblemon/reference/homies/optimize.mjs`. Layout dimensions and insets come from the generated pixels, not speculative CSS boxes.

At landscape heights of 560px or less, a uniformly scaled tablet leaves too little screen width for full contact rows. That breakpoint uses a nine-slice border made from the same generated tablet file: source slices 114 / 185 / 116 / 189, displayed as 28px top/bottom and 36px side rails. Only straight rails stretch; the original corners remain intact, with no filled center and no whole-image distortion. Live controls sit 2px inside the border's content box and scroll there. The normal phone/tablet paths retain their uniform scale and the published pixel bounds. A failed raster retains the HTML screen and its actions.
