# SQUABBLEMON ART — TRANSPARENT COMPLETE PACK

This pack merges the uploaded `sQUABBLEMON ART.zip` with the newest Squabblemon art/effect assets from this conversation.

## Background cleanup
- Near-white backgrounds connected to the outside edge were converted to transparency.
- White clothing, highlights, labels, eyes, shoes, and interior details are preserved because the cleanup is edge-connected rather than a global “delete white” operation.
- Existing dark/non-white backgrounds are left intact.
- Animated orb GIFs were processed frame-by-frame and remain animated.
- Ground/contact shadows are intentionally retained where they are darker than the background.
