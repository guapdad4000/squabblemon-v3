## Logline

> A neighborhood block party turns into a five-act family war when
> Cracked Head — believed dead for four years — returns to claim the
> block his younger brother Ganger Blue has been holding in his place.

---

## Theme

**The block is a family heirloom, and family is the heaviest card in the deck.**

Each chapter tests that theme from a different angle:
- Turf (Chapter 1) — what is the block worth?
- Family (Chapter 2) — who are you protecting it for?
- Loyalty (Chapter 3) — what does loyalty cost?
- Secrets (Chapter 4) — what happens when the secrets get out?
- Sacrifice (Chapter 5) — what are you willing to lose?

## Story-mode loop (per chapter)

1. Player enters the chapter map.
2. Sees the chapter title + subtitle, the optional mastery routes, and the
   recommended first battle.
3. Taps the first battle. Sees the **BattleBriefing** — enemy intel, the
   3-star objectives, the recommended collection, and the dialogue hook.
4. Plays the battle.
5. On the **ResultScreen**, sees the 3 stars and the ticket earned
   (1 per 3-star clean sweep). 3-star payout is automatic.
6. Returns to the chapter map. Sees the next node unlock.
7. Repeats until the chapter is cleared.
8. On the **chapter finale** node, sees the full reward list — the chapter
   key, the card back, the character unlock, and the bonus tickets.
9. The next chapter unlocks via the `chapter-key` reward.

---

## Tickets math

- 1 perfect battle = 1 ticket (auto).
- 1 long-form chapter (7 battles) = 7 tickets if perfect.
- 1 compact chapter (3 battles) = 3 tickets if perfect.
- 1 full season (8 chapters, ~50 battles) = 49 tickets if perfect.
- 1 chapter finale = 1 explicit ticket (always granted, even on imperfect
  play that completes the chapter) + 1 chapter key + cosmetic + character unlock.

The ticket economy is generous on a perfect run, punishing on a sloppy
run. Players who replay chapters for stars earn tickets at a comfortable
clip; players who skip mastery are forced to grind.

## Writing cadence

1. Lock the **season-arc** first.
2. Lock the **character bible** for the four corner bosses (Cracked Head,
   OG Uncle, Baby Momma, Snitch) and the two leads (Ganger Blue, Ganger Red).
3. Flesh out the supporting cast files (one per character, 1-2 pages each).
4. Write the chapter screenplays in order. Each chapter takes one focused
   writing session; expect ~3-4 hours per long-form chapter and ~1-2 hours
   per compact-ticket chapter.
5. Hand off to the director. Director returns with parallax PNGs and any
   pacing notes. Writer revises once.

## Open questions to settle before writing

- [ ] Does OG Uncle die in chapter 5 or live to chapter 8?
- [ ] Does Ganger Blue betray Cracked Head at the end, or vice versa?
- [ ] Is Baby Momma Cracked Head's ex, or his sister?
- [ ] Does the player ever fight Techbro Rich's deck, or just watch from
      the rooftop?
- [ ] What's Snitch's price for keeping quiet about Ganger Blue's snitching?
- [ ] Does Cracked Head's "return" mean he's alive the whole time, or is it
      a ghost / reputation only?

The writer picks. The director executes. The player feels.
