# SEASON 1 — `block-party`

> The first season of Squabblemon Story Mode. Three acts, eight chapters.
> Long-form chapters carry the drama; compact-ticket chapters deliver
> the 3-battle rhythm so the player feels the loop. Total: ~50 battles,
> 49 tickets on a perfect run.

---

## Logline

> A neighborhood block party turns into a five-act family war when
> Cracked Head — believed dead for four years — returns to claim the
> block his younger brother Ganger Blue has been holding in his place.

---

## Acts

### Act I — The Block (chapters 1-2)

- The player arrives. Establishes the block, the two lieutenants
  (Ganger Blue, Ganger Red), the boss (Cracked Head), and the rumor
  mill (Snitch).
- The block is up for grabs. Two chapters, two rivalries, one boss.
- Cliffhanger: Cracked Head says "I'll be back."

### Act II — The Family (chapters 3-5)

- A compact-ticket chapter on Blue Side, then a long-form Snitch
  chapter, then the OG Uncle reveal.
- Cracked Head's "death" is questioned. Baby Momma shows up and
  claims Cracked Head is the father of her kid.
- Cliffhanger: Cracked Head walks into the corner store alive.

### Act III — The Crown (chapters 6-8)

- The function, the return, the season finale. Compact + long-form +
  long-form, in that order, because the player should feel the loop
  pick up speed as the climax approaches.
- Cracked Head vs Ganger Blue, 1v1, 3 phases. The block changes hands.
- Cliffhanger for season 2: Techbro Rich buys the rooftop.

## Story-mode loop (per chapter)

1. Player enters the chapter map.
2. Sees the chapter title + subtitle, optional mastery routes, recommended first battle.
3. Taps the first battle → **BattleBriefing**.
4. Plays the battle.
5. **ResultScreen** shows 3 stars + the ticket earned (1 per 3-star clean sweep).
6. Returns to chapter map. Next node unlocks.
7. Repeats until the chapter is cleared.
8. On the **chapter finale** node, sees the full reward list — chapter key,
   card back, character unlock, bonus tickets.
9. The next chapter unlocks via the `chapter-key` reward.

## Drama call sheet (writer-to-writer)

- **Chapter 1:** Establish the world. Make the player feel like a guest at
  a block party where two gangs are measuring dicks. End on a cliffhanger
  — Cracked Head says "I'll be back" over the rooftop.
- **Chapter 2:** Make the player pick a side. Ganger Red and Baby Momma
  are the heart; Ganger Blue and Wifey are the alternative. End with
  Baby Momma playing a card from Cracked Head's old deck (a clue).
- **Chapter 3:** Compact. Three battles, three characters, three secrets.
  OG Uncle is sick. Ganger Blue won't visit. Wifey makes him go.
- **Chapter 4:** Snitch sells fake receipts. The player has to figure out
  which is real. Church Auntie officiates a fake funeral; it's not for
  Cracked Head. (It is.)
- **Chapter 5:** OG Uncle reveals Cracked Head is alive. Baby Momma drops
  the kid bombshell. Wifey stands by Blue. Church Auntie blesses the
  truth. The player has to fight OG Uncle (2 phases) to earn the chapter.
- **Chapter 6:** Compact. The function. Every character who's still alive
  is in the room. Bottle Girl runs the bar. Promoter books the fight.
  Live Streamer narrates. Three fights, three tickets. The party ends
  with Cracked Head walking in.
- **Chapter 7:** The return. Cracked Head is back. Ganger Blue has to
  face him. Snitch is there. OG Uncle is in a wheelchair. Baby Momma
  is pregnant with Cracked Head's second kid. 8 battles, 8 tickets.
- **Chapter 8:** The crown. Three-phase boss fight: the block itself.
  Whoever wins the rooftop gets the season. 9 battles, 9 tickets. End
  with Techbro Rich buying the rooftop for season 2.

## Resolved-decision log (do not re-open)

These six decisions are now canon for season 1. Any chapter screenplay that
contradicts them must be revised, not the decisions.

1. **OG Uncle lives to chapter 8.** He is present (in a wheelchair from
   chapter 7 onward) for the finale. He does NOT die in chapter 5.
   Implications for chapter 5 ("Old Heads Know"): the 2-phase OG Uncle
   boss fight is a test of truth, not a death scene. He walks out alive.
2. **Ganger Blue betrays Cracked Head in chapter 7 and redeems in chapter 8.**
   Chapter 7's "Return of the Block" beats: Blue takes the deal, Cracked Head
   walks in, the lie is exposed. Chapter 8: Blue fights for the block anyway
   and earns his name back.
3. **Baby Momma is Cracked Head's ex.** She is not his sister. She has one
   child by him at the start of season 1 (toddler, off-screen), and a second
   on the way by chapter 7.
4. **Techbro Rich does not duel the player in season 1.** He buys the rooftop
   at the end of chapter 8 and is the season 2 setup. His deck is shown but
   never fought.
5. **Snitch sells to everyone.** He has no fixed loyalty. Every chapter can
   cast him as either helper or obstacle depending on who paid more that
   week. His only consistent motivation is the camera roll.
6. **Cracked Head is alive the entire season.** His "death" was a faked
   witness-protection move arranged by OG Uncle. Ganger Blue discovered the
   arrangement and Snitch spread the rumor that Cracked Head died, forcing
   OG Uncle to lean on the feds to make it stick. Blue's betrayal in chapter
   7 is his guilt catching up. Cracked Head walks into the corner store in
   chapter 7's finale, alive.
