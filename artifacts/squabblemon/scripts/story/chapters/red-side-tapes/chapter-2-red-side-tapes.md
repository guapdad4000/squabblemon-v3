## 1. Dramatic spine

Play the old message immediately. Red's concealed note proves Cracked Head knew about their child; both men have to face Baby Momma's questions. Nobody has to win a card match to make that message true.

The next day's already-booked Red Side Gauntlet offers a concrete player goal: clear six posted tables to host the next neighborhood showcase. This is a new event role. The Chapter One Crown and equal-access schedule remain earned and intact. All six opponents are on the lineup before the first match; the chapter never invents a fresh gatekeeper after each victory.

The fights give the family breathing room and opportunities to make things worse. Cornball starts a cheese subscription. Snitch sells seats facing a private door. Roaster confuses protecting his sister with selling tickets to his jokes. Wifey gets a rare chance to play while Baby Momma handles check-in. Baby Momma is the final opponent because she wants event leadership, not because the player can win her family decisions.

The final win earns the host card. Red then takes Baby Momma to the man who directed his silence: OG Uncle. The chapter cuts on her question, “Then who exactly were you protecting?” The rescue and the address leak remain later reveals.

## 5. Setup / payoff and custody of evidence

- Recorder stays with Baby Momma from Scene 1 through the porch. No theft or new copy is used to manufacture another chase.
- Full message in Scene 1 → Red owns the four-year omission in Scene 5 → OG Uncle owns his instruction in Scene 9.
- No Family Questions sign → check-in sign → actual family answers away from the public camera.
- Cornball's cheese company → breathing dispenser → rejected vendor application → Auntie's funeral for the dispenser joke.
- Roaster sells protection as performance → carries food as actual support.
- Wifey gets a seat to play → Baby Momma trusts her → Wifey cannot meet that trust cleanly yet.
- Cracked Head brings a chair as a grand gesture → is asked to put it with the other chairs.
- Untouched place setting in Scene 5 → reserved for Chapter Eight's first meaningful parenting commitment.
- Snitch records Red saying he knew in Scene 4. This public event clip is distinct from the private voice note and from the warehouse footage used later in the season.
