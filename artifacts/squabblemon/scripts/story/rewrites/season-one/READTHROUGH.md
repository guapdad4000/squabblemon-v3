# Season One — playable dialogue rewrite

Same eight chapters, 62 nodes, art, opponents and rewards. Current spoken screenplay; human-readable source for the generated game dialogue.

The player arrives to win a place for their crew in the neighborhood tournament. The former champion, believed dead for four years, returns at the first final. His brother runs the events now; his former partner has raised their child alone. As the tournament continues, the player becomes the one competitor both sides trust to stay and listen. Each chapter must say where we are, why we are playing, and what has changed before sending us onward.
