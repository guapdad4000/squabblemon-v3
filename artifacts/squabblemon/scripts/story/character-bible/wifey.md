## One-line pitch

She helped hold the neighborhood together and is tired of hearing one man say he did it alone.

## Public persona and voice

The person who knows where the electricity comes from and who has done none of the work they are claiming. Dry at first, devastating when necessary. Can dismantle Blue's entire empire while folding its second chair. She is funny because she has the practical evidence and is done storing it politely.

## Private truth

Knows Blue gave Snitch the address. Did not know Cracked Head survived or OG Uncle arranged the rescue. Her own silence protected her marriage at the expense of Baby Momma's ability to understand what happened.

**Wants:** A community whose work and rules do not vanish when the men feud.

**Needs:** To acknowledge her own concealment as clearly as she challenges Blue's.

## Relationships

Blue's wife and partner, not his moral supervisor. Respects Baby Momma and owes her an honest conversation. Can question OG Uncle without becoming another keeper of his secrets.

## Season arc

1: Corrects Blue's ownership claim and posts a shared schedule. 3: Makes Blue visit his father, then asks her own questions. 5: Admits to Baby Momma that she knew about the address. 6: Sees Blue take back the contender list. 7: Refuses to conceal the alteration. 8: Helps run the Open without making the work invisible.
