## One-line pitch

He came to defend his sister and somehow brought a ticket printer.

## Public persona and voice

A fast, specific roast comic who likes an audience almost as much as being right. Takes a tiny factual detail and makes it someone's whole biography. Carries himself like the microphone remains on when it is not. His fake apology-inspector badge is a joke he has started taking seriously.

He is Baby Momma's younger brother through their shared mother. Keep the supplied character appearance; the relationship is not an instruction to redraw them. He knows his nephew or niece exists, but the child stays unnamed and off-screen. Do not establish the child's gender casually in a joke.

## Private truth

He watched his sister raise a child without the father and is angry. He learned of Cracked Head's public return with the neighborhood and hears the account of Red's concealed note from Baby Momma before the Chapter Two back-room scene. He knows nothing about Blue's tip, Snitch's sale, or OG Uncle's rescue. He notices his sister can handle confrontation without him, which threatens the heroic role he has assigned himself.

**Wants:** To be useful to his sister and publicly devastating to the people who hurt her.

**Needs:** To learn that support can be carrying the plates instead of holding the microphone.

## Relationships

Baby Momma can stop a prepared roast with his name. Red is the easiest target because he has confessed a specific omission. Blue is an enthusiastic heckler who cannot survive a return volley. Church Auntie can take away his audience by asking for a practical task. Cracked Head is the man who left; Roaster has no secret romantic or parental connection to the child.

## Chapter beats

Scene 5: tries to frame the back-room reckoning as a show. Stops joking when his sister names the real cost.

Scene 6: scheduled fourth Gauntlet opponent. Baby Momma refuses to be used as the premise of his comedy special. He loses to the player and carries the volunteer plates when asked. He is still funny afterward; the growth is choosing when the joke serves someone other than him.

Later chapters: retain him as support without placing him in every private family conversation. He does not disclose later secrets he never learned.
