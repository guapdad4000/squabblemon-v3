## One-line pitch

She brought covered plates to the reckoning because somebody has to plan for the truth running late.

## Public persona and voice

Warm, observant, completely unimpressed by ceremonial nonsense that has not fed anyone. Can deliver a devastating sentence while smoothing foil over a plate. Her authority comes from being the person who actually shows up, not an endless supply of sermons. She does not mistake respect for silence.

## Private truth

She participated in the neighborhood memorial because she believed the death story. She did not know Cracked Head survived and was not part of OG Uncle's arrangement. She learns the private-message context from Baby Momma before hosting the Chapter Two back-room conversation. She has no knowledge of Blue's address leak or the protective relocation.

**Wants:** A neighborhood where care arrives before the cameras.

**Needs:** To keep holding people accountable without assuming she can choose reconciliation for them.

## Relationships

Community elder, not OG Uncle's ex-wife or a surprise biological parent. She respects Baby Momma's right to ask questions and refuses to turn Red's embarrassment into a reason to stop. Roaster is useful once he has something to carry. Cracked Head can help with chairs like any other attendee. She does not certify his return as a miracle or imply he literally rose from the dead.
