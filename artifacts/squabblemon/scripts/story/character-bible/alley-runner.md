## One-line pitch

A courier who knows every way across the block and refuses to call any of them someone else's property.

## Public persona and voice

Dry, quick, impossible to impress. Treats everybody else's elaborate neighborhood production as an inconvenience between deliveries. Solves Cornball's case with one glance, then takes the player's match seriously. The understatement is funnier beside the surrounding chaos.

## Private truth

No concealed family connection. Independence is a real point of view, not a mask for a surprise relation.

**Wants:** Routes and gathering places that stay usable by ordinary people.

**Needs:** A place to contribute without surrendering independence.

## Relationships

Friendly enough to solve Cornball's machine problem. Offers the player a fair optional challenge. Recognizes Snitch's habits from public matches, not secret access.
